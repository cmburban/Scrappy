# Scrappy brand kit

Neighbor-to-neighbor art and craft supply sharing. Everything here was created for the Scrappy prototype. The logo, corgi and illustrations are original artwork.

## Folders
- `logo/svg` – vector logos (scale to any size, best for print)
- `logo/png` – ready-to-use images (transparent unless the name says "bg")
- `illustrations/svg|png` – 12 supply pictures (paper, paint, yarn, floss, fabric, beads, clay, tools, linocut, frames, washi, other), 4:3
- `brand/` – this guide

## Which logo to use
- **scrappy-logo-horizontal-color** – default, on light backgrounds
- **scrappy-logo-horizontal-white** – on dark or photo backgrounds (transparent, so it looks blank on white)
- **scrappy-logo-horizontal-on-dark-bg / on-cream-bg** – same, with the background included
- **scrappy-corgi-head** – profile photo, favicon, stickers
- **scrappy-app-icon** – rounded app-style icon
- **scrappy-corgi-mascot-sitting** – full-body mascot with a ball of yarn, for posters and social posts

Keep clear space around the logo of at least the height of the corgi's ear. Don't stretch it or recolor the corgi.

## Fonts (free, SIL Open Font License, from Google Fonts)
- **Bricolage Grotesque** Bold – logo wordmark and headlines
- **Figtree** – body text
- **DM Mono** – small uppercase labels

The wordmark in the logo files is already converted to shapes, so you don't need the font installed to use the logo.

## Tone
Friendly and neighborly, plain words: "give away", "swap", "spare supplies".
